AVA Arena

Competition mode - gamified T1D management for families

Idea / Design Phase

💡 The Concept

A 5-inch touchscreen display showing 2-4 T1D family members competing on blood sugar management. Weekly championships, daily scores, and a crown for the winner. Turn diabetes management into a family game.

🎯 Scoring System (Points per Hour)

Zone BG Range Points Vibe
🎯 Target70-140+100Crushing it
✅ In Range140-180+70Solid
⚠️ Mild High180-250+20Happens
🔶 High250-3000Neutral
🔴 Very High300+-10Gentle nudge
⚠️ Mild Low55-70+10Caught it!
🔴 Severe Low<55-20Safety

Philosophy: Don't punish highs/lows too hard - they already deal with enough. Always earnable points.

🏅 Competition Modes

  • ⚡
    Quick Match - 24 hours
  • 🎮
    Weekend Warrior - 72 hours
  • 👑
    Weekly Championship - 7 days, winner gets crown displayed all next week

✨ Bonus Points

  • 🦸
    Recovery Hero - +50 for getting back in range within 1 hour after high/low
  • 🌙
    Night Owl - +25 bonus for overnight (11pm-6am) time in range
  • 📈
    Steady Eddie - +30 for 2+ hours with no swings >30 points
