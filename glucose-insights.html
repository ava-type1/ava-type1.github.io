📱 Export from Glooko Recommended — includes insulin + carb data!

1
Open Glooko on your computer
Go to my.glooko.com and log in with your Glooko account
2
Click on your profile icon
Top-right corner → then click "Export Data" or "Download"
3
Pick your date range
We recommend at least 14 days for the best insights (30-90 days is ideal)
4
Download the ZIP file
It'll save as something like glooko_export.zip — don't unzip it!
✓
Drop the ZIP file right here!
We'll automatically extract your CGM readings, insulin doses, carbs, basal rates, and more
💡 On your phone? Open my.glooko.com in Safari/Chrome, export there, then come back and tap the upload area to select the ZIP from your Downloads.

🟢 Export from Dexcom Clarity

1
Open Dexcom Clarity
Go to clarity.dexcom.com and sign in
2
Click the Export button
Look for the Export icon (download arrow) in the top-right area of the dashboard
3
Select date range and download
Choose your time period and click "Export" — saves as a CSV file
✓
Upload the CSV file here!
Drop it in the upload area above or tap to browse your files

🔵 Export from FreeStyle Libre / LibreView

1
Open LibreView
Go to libreview.com and sign in with your account
2
Go to Reports
Click on the Reports tab, then look for "Download Glucose Data" or the export/download button
3
Download the CSV
Select your date range and export — it downloads as a CSV file
✓
Upload the CSV file here!
Drop it in the upload area above — we support both Libre 2 and Libre 3 formats

Experimental Lab 🧪

Advanced analytics powered by your glucose data. These features are in active development — expect magic and the occasional rough edge.

🍽️ Meal Impact Analyzer Alpha

Auto-detects likely meals by finding glucose spikes >30 mg/dL within 30 minutes. Scores each meal by spike magnitude, time to peak, and recovery time. All computed from your CGM data — no food logging needed.
📊Upload glucose data above to see meal analysis

🌅 Dawn Phenomenon Detector Alpha

Analyzes your 4 AM – 8 AM glucose rise compared to your overnight baseline (midnight–4 AM). Identifies whether you experience dawn phenomenon and how consistently it occurs.
🌙Upload glucose data above to detect dawn phenomenon

🏃 Exercise Impact Window Beta

Detects periods of rapid glucose drop (>2 mg/dL per minute sustained for 10+ minutes) which likely correlate with physical activity. Shows timing patterns and average glucose impact.
🏃Upload glucose data above to detect exercise windows

📊 Weekly Report Card Beta

Breaks your data into weekly chunks and grades each week (A–F) based on Time in Range, low events, high events, and glucose variability. See your trend over time.
📋Upload glucose data above to see your weekly grades

🔮 "What If" Simulator Coming Soon

Use interactive sliders to model how changes to your insulin settings might affect your glucose. Shift your BG curve by adjusting basal rate or pre-bolus timing — based on your actual data.
