⚠️ Low Blood Sugar + Driving = Danger

Distracted driving from hypoglycemia is a real risk. AVA products are designed to keep glucose visible when your phone isn't accessible — so you can get home safe.

Products Built for Real Life

From the car to the classroom, AVA keeps you connected to your glucose levels — and helps kids learn along the way.

🚗 Coming Q1 2026

Ava Mini

A tiny 1.47" glucose display that goes where your phone can't. Mount it in your car, clip it to the treadmill, take it to the theme park.

  • Connects to Dexcom CGM
  • Ultra-portable design
  • Glanceable from any angle
  • No phone fumbling required
📱 Available Now

AVA Type 1 App

An educational simulation that teaches kids (ages 5-18) how to manage diabetes through games, challenges, and rewards — not lectures. Built by a T1D dad for his T1D daughter.

  • Realistic T1D simulation gameplay
  • Carb counting, insulin dosing, highs & lows
  • Achievement system & stickers
  • Safe simulation — not real medical data
  • Pairs with AVA Companion via Bluetooth

AVA Companion

A friendly plush toy with a built-in display that shows glucose levels. Because sometimes a stuffed friend makes it all less scary.

  • Kid-safe design
  • BLE connected to CGM
  • Comforting companion for young T1Ds
  • Pairs with the AVA app
