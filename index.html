The App

Meet AVA Type 1

An interactive diabetes companion that makes learning fun. Simulation gameplay, mini-games, adaptive education, and a world that grows with your child.

Simulation

Live Glucose Simulation

Watch AVA's world react to blood glucose in real time. Fish swim, storms brew, gardens bloom — all tied to BG levels. Learning through immersion, not lectures.

Games

Glucose Range Games

Color-coded challenges teach kids what different BG numbers mean. Tap, drag, and play your way to understanding — no textbook required.

Education

Age-Adaptive Learning

Four levels — Kids, Teen, Adult, and Technical — so content matches the learner. Track progress and master T1D concepts at your own pace.

Personalize

6 Immersive Themes

Dark Fractal, Glucose Wave, Cellular, Storm, Ocean, Garden — pick the world that fits your mood. Plus BLE companion device pairing built right in.

Real Story

Why I Built AVA Drive

My brother Cody left for work one morning — a routine 30-minute drive. He never made it. A low blood sugar episode behind the wheel led to this. His family spent hours searching, not knowing where he was or if he was okay.

This is preventable. If someone in the car could see his glucose dropping — a simple number on a display — they could have pulled over. He could have treated. The crash never happens.

"Every person with T1D who drives is one bad low away from this. AVA Drive exists so no family has to post this kind of message ever again."

— Kam, founder of AVA Type 1
